About the Company
Our client is a highly successful, mission-driven legal technology platform founded in Melbourne with a global footprint. They provide a no-code builder and an advanced AI module that empowers enterprise legal teams and community legal centres to turn complex processes into accessible, self-service tools.
  
With a deeply ingrained culture of purpose, scrappiness, and support, this small but mighty team of 20 is revolutionising how legal work gets done. They believe strongly in remote work—whether you're based in a major city or driving around Australia in a van. They maintain connection through regular all-hands meetings and fantastic team offsites (past trips include Bali, Vietnam, and the Great Ocean Road). Plus, every full-time employee enjoys one dedicated "Company Day" (a day dedicated to the company's core mission).
  
The Role
We are seeking an innovative and strategic AI Engineer to join a flat-structured engineering team of five, reporting directly to the CEO. This is a greenfield, exploratory role where you will play a pivotal part in transitioning the company toward an AI-native delivery workflow.
  
You don't need decades of commercial AI experience; instead, we are looking for a curious, product-minded engineer who understands business problems and user needs. You will help build the orchestration layer that connects the company's core workflow builder with its new AI product, integrating with internal and external agents.
  
Key Responsibilities
Strategic Implementation: Work closely with the Tech Leads and CEO to implement the technical vision, moving experimental AI prototypes into robust, production-ready features.
Agent Orchestration: Design and build overarching orchestration capabilities, managing APIs and Model Context Protocols (MCPs) to expose products to other agents.
Product Development: Contribute to the evolution of the company's two key products: the original no-code workflow builder and the strict, hallucination-free AI Q&A layer.
Process Innovation: Help review and elevate the way the engineering team builds software, integrating AI tools (like Claude Code) to enhance coding, design, and delivery workflows.
Continuous Improvement: Call out gaps in the current architecture (currently GCP, Python, TypeScript, Vue) and guide the team in establishing AI-driven DevOps practices.
  
About You
Mindset Over Tech Stack: You possess a strong product mindset. While experience with Python, TypeScript, Vue, and GCP is highly advantageous, your attitude, curiosity, and openness to learning are what truly matter.
AI Enthusiasm: You are an experienced and curious, mid-to-senior-level engineer with a deep interest in AI orchestration, agents, and LLMs.
Strategic Thinker: You can understand complex business problems and take others on the journey with you, translating research and prototypes into meaningful outcomes.
Adaptable: You thrive in a startup environment 
Empathy for the User: You care about the end-user experience, specifically making life better and more efficient for legal teams.
