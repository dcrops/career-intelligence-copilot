Carlton Football Club has made a deliberate and significant investment in AI, with a genuine appetite for innovation from the top down, and the returns are already visible across the Club. Working closely with the GM of Data, Technology and AI, the AI Enablement Lead will be responsible for driving adoption and education across every part of the Club. This newly created role is one of the first of its kind in Australian sport, and provides an opportunity to play a highly influential role in the transformation of an elite sporting organisation. 

The role is ultimately about people, and is best suited to someone who genuinely loves helping others learn. The focus is on identifying where AI can improve how staff work, removing the barriers that stop adoption, and building the skills and confidence of the Club to embrace it responsibly. 

Looking further ahead, the Club is ambitious about what AI can do. From agentic workflows to organisation-wide transformation, the roadmap beyond the first 12 months is compelling, and this role is where that journey begins. 

Education and Adoption 

Design and lead Carlton's AI learning program across all departments, structured workshops, regular training sessions, and drop-in support tailored to staff at every level 
Build and maintain a thriving internal AI community, keeping staff informed, inspired, and connected to what others are building and learning across the Club 
Manage and enable Carlton's AI champions network, ensuring champions across every department feel supported, capable, and are producing output with visible business impact 
Own and run Carlton's AI adoption tracking program, surfacing patterns in usage, identifying blockers, and ensuring every insight leads to a closed-out action 
Support all users across the Club, paid and free, ensuring no one is left behind regardless of which tools they have access to 
 Building 

Develop and maintain Carlton's AI playbook and internal resources, keeping them current and genuinely useful as the Club's primary reference for AI at work 
Build prompt libraries, AI-powered workflows, and lightweight tools that solve real problems across the business, with a focus on outcomes that have clear business value 
Work closely with the Data and Technology teams to identify AI integration opportunities, including tools with AI capabilities built in, connectors, and workflow solutions 
 Exploration 

Continuously scan the AI market for emerging tools, platforms, and techniques, evaluating what is worth adopting and bringing clear recommendations back to the team 
Identify common themes and recurring gaps in how staff work, and explore whether agentic workflows or AI-driven solutions could address them at scale 
Prototype and test new AI approaches, including agentic solutions, that could meaningfully change how the Club operates, keeping Carlton ahead of the curve as the technology matures 
 Governance 

Embed safe and responsible AI use at the core of all education, training materials, and resources delivered to staff 
Own Carlton's AI Use Policy as a living document and maintain governance guides that ensure AI is used responsibly and in line with the Club's values 
 AI Strategy and Leadership 

Deliver regular AI adoption reporting to the executive leadership team and contribute ground-level insight to Carlton's broader AI strategy and roadmap 
Track the AI landscape, flagging relevant emerging tools, opportunities, and risks to senior leadership on an ongoing basis 
 Who We Are Looking For 

You might have come from education, change management, consulting, tech enablement, or somewhere else entirely. What matters is that you can bring people on a journey with AI, and you have the hands-on experience to back it up. 


You will be a strong fit if you 

Have hands-on experience building with AI, including workflows and automations in a real organisational context 
Are highly proficient with Claude or other reputable AI tools, and are always exploring what else is emerging in the market 
Have designed and delivered training or capability programs for technology or AI adoption 
Can translate a business problem into a practical, outcomes-focused AI solution 
Communicate clearly with people at every level, from frontline staff to executives 
Have genuine intellectual curiosity about AI and a bias toward doing over theorising 
Are comfortable in an environment where not everything is defined yet, and see that as an opportunity 
Have an interest in Australian Rules Football or experience working in an elite sporting organisation, preferred but not essential
